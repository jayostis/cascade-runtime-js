EFF Long Wordlist (eff_large_wordlist.txt)
==========================================

Source:  https://www.eff.org/files/2016/07/18/eff_large_wordlist.txt
Author:  Joseph Bonneau, for the Electronic Frontier Foundation (2016)
License: Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 United States (CC BY 3.0 US)
         https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/us/

Used by `six-words.ts` to make the six words that open an encrypted pod.

`eff-wordlist.ts` embeds the list verbatim, one entry per line with its
five-digit dice column, so it can be compared with EFF's published copy:
7776 entries (6^5), md5 fcb9fd13e5f6512a790553aefff54f10. `six-words.ts` checks
the whole 11111..66666 sequence, and that every word is distinct and matches
^[a-z-]+$, on first use.
